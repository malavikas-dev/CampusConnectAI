About CampusConnect AI

Reconnecting people with their belongings through the power of Artificial Intelligence.

🎯 Our Mission

CampusConnect AI is an intelligent Lost & Found platform built to simplify the recovery of misplaced belongings within educational institutions. Instead of relying on traditional notice boards or social media posts, the platform provides a secure, organized and AI-assisted solution for reporting, matching and recovering lost items.

🚀 Key Features

  • 🤖 AI-powered Lost & Found Matching
  • 🔐 Secure User Authentication
  • 📷 Image Upload for Items
  • 💬 Private Chat between Owner & Finder
  • 📩 Claim Management System
  • 🔔 Notifications & Email Alerts
  • 📊 Admin Dashboard

💡 Why CampusConnect AI?

Every year, countless personal belongings are misplaced on campuses, making recovery difficult due to scattered communication channels. CampusConnect AI centralizes the entire process, making it faster, smarter, and more reliable for both students and staff.
